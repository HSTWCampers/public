Privacy Policy — HSTW Campers

Last updated: 8 June 2026

HSTW Campers ("the app") is a personal tool used by the owner of the HSTW Campers social-media accounts to organise his own photos and videos of his self-build camper and to publish that content to his own social-media accounts (including TikTok, Instagram and Facebook). This policy explains what data the app handles.

Who this is for

The app is operated privately by the account owner for his own accounts. It is not a public service and does not create accounts for, or process data of, other people.

What data we access

  • Your own media: photos and videos you add on your own computer, which you choose to publish.
  • TikTok account access: when you connect your TikTok account, the app uses TikTok's Content Posting API only to upload and publish videos that you have created, on your behalf.
  • Basic post metrics: public counts such as views, likes and comments of your own posts, to show performance.

How we use it

Solely to let you manage your own content and publish it to your own accounts, and to display how your posts perform. We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Where data is stored

Your media and settings are stored locally on your own computer. Access tokens for connected accounts are stored locally and used only to communicate with the respective platform's official API.

Data retention & deletion

Because data lives on your own device, you can delete it at any time by removing the files or revoking the app's access in your TikTok account settings. Revoking access immediately stops any further use of your TikTok data.

Third-party platforms

Publishing uses the official APIs of TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. Their handling of your data is governed by their own privacy policies.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach the operator via Instagram: @hstwcampers.
